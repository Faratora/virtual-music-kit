About Me

Hi! I’m Vera, a photographer with 5 years of experience. For me, photography is more than just an image. It’s a way to feel the moment, capture the atmosphere, and freeze time. I work with people, stories, and emotions.

I shoot all across Europe. I love natural light, genuine emotions, and beautiful details. Each shot is a small piece of art. My goal is to create timeless images that tell your unique story.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Wanted to Ask (and a Bit More)

HOW DO I BOOK A SHOOT?

Super simple – just message me through any convenient platform (Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, or the contact form on this site). We’ll discuss your preferred date, city, and session type. Once confirmed, I’ll reserve the date for you and send over preparation tips.

Can I choose the location myself?

Absolutely! I’ll also suggest options based on your style and story.

How long does the shoot take?

Depends on the package — from 1 to 4 hours including breaks.

When will I receive the photos?

Usually within 7–14 days, depending on the package.

Will you help me pose?

Of course! I guide you throughout the shoot to make you feel confident and natural.

What should I wear?

I’ll send styling tips and help you choose outfits that match the mood.

Do you retouch the photos?

I’ll send styling tips and help you choose outfits that match the mood.

Can I get the raw files?

RAW files are not included by default, but can be discussed individually.
